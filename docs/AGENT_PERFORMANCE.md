# Narrated performances

[Home](../README.md) · [Agent connection](CONNECT_AGENT.md) · [Example script](../examples/vaelari_performance.json)

Use `valkyrie-perform` when you want Vaelari to speak and move through a long video script. Start Studio, load your model, open **Voice**, choose a provider and voice, and leave Studio running. In **Voice → Make a long video**, copy the complete example JSON, save it as `my-video.json`, and edit the lines. You can also copy [the example file](../examples/vaelari_performance.json). Run `valkyrie-perform --script my-video.json --validate` first; this checks the script without generating speech or recording. Then run `valkyrie-perform --script my-video.json --output my-video.mp4` from the package folder.

For Eleven v3, set top-level `"provider": "elevenlabs"` and `"tts_model": "eleven_v3"`. In each line, set `emotion` and `expression_intensity` (0–5). For example, `{"text":"I found it!","emotion":"excited","expression_intensity":3,"gesture":"nod","motion":[{"at":0,"yaw":-8},{"at":1,"yaw":6}]}` sends `[excited]` to Eleven v3, starts an excited facial expression and nod when that line begins, and turns the head during its measured audio. `emotion` may be `neutral`, `joy`, `excited`, `curious`, `thinking`, `sad`, `angry`, or `surprised`. Without an explicit `gesture`, Valkyrie chooses a matching nod, tilt, shake, or lean. Omit `motion` for natural generated head movement. Split a long paragraph into shorter lines when the emotion changes; this keeps voice and model cues aligned at a clear boundary. You may also put Eleven v3 delivery tags such as `[whispers]` in the line text, while `emotion` remains the source of the model's facial cue. [ElevenLabs describes these audio tags as guidance](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/best-practices/prompting), so the exact vocal tone still depends on the selected voice.

The script accepts `provider` (`elevenlabs` or `openai`), optional top-level `tts_model` and `model_path`, even `width`/`height`, `fps`, `view` (`zoom`, `x`, `y`), `motion_energy` (0–2, default 1.35), `chapter_seconds`, `lead_in`, `tail`, and 1–1000 `lines`. Each line needs `text`; optional fields are `voice`, line-level `tts_model`, `emotion`, `expression_intensity`, `gesture`, `pause`, `motion`, and `audio`. A line-level speech model overrides the top-level model. `gesture` can be `nod`, `shake`, `tilt`, or `lean` and starts with that line. `pause` defaults to **0.18 seconds** after that line, including the final line. For the closest natural joins, use 0.12–0.22 seconds and keep lead-in/tail at zero. A line's `audio` can point to a local WAV/MP3 instead of spending TTS credits; `text` is still required as its script label. With existing audio, model emotion and movement still follow the line, but the tool cannot change the prerecorded vocal delivery.

A `motion` cue uses `at` from 0.0 to 1.0 across that line's measured audio, plus yaw/pitch/roll in degrees. Studio interpolates between cues and fades head motion through the short pause. If omitted, it makes larger, line-varied head movements; the agent puppeteer adds body sway, gaze, and blinking between cues. A `gesture` adds a brief reaction without interrupting the line. Mouth motion follows the audio, so do not put jaw or mouth parameters in head cues. The runner prepares every line, trims obvious edge silence, composes audio for each chapter, starts recording and audio together, applies each line's facial emotion at its start, sends head tracking at 20 Hz, waits for the final encode, and joins chapter files into one video. It restores the previous live motion-energy setting after a successful recording. The working folder next to your output keeps prepared WAV and chapter files for inspection. Choose a fresh output path for another run.

For `.mp4` use an FFmpeg build with `utvideo` and `libx264`; `.webm` needs `utvideo` and `libvpx-vp9`; `.mov` needs `utvideo` and `prores_ks`. Export may take longer than playback time. The runner reports a failed chapter rather than silently truncating the video. On the Windows test workstation, a four-line, two-chapter WebM was checked end to end; longer runs remain unverified. See [Validation](../VALIDATION.md).
